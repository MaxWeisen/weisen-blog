# My First Blog Post

Published on: 2025-01-19

Welcome to my _new blog_ built with Astro.

## Goals For This Blog

1. **Learn New Technology**: I am learning how to use Astro. Building is the way that works best for me when starting out with a new tool. My ultimate goal is to learn Astro's best practices and how to leverage these learnings into building an easy to build and deploy blog.

2. **Sharing Space**: I would like to have a place to share knowledge and opinions I have about web development and software.

3. **Trying Something New**: I've never had a blog before. Typically I write down ideas and plans on a sheet of paper. With this blog I plan to move these ideas into a public digital space.
